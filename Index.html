Shriram B. Dawange | Founder & CEO, Gen Solution

The Introduction

"I am Shriram B. Dawange, and I believe in building ecosystems that change how we interact with the world. I don't just write code; I engineer scalable solutions to complex real-world problems. Currently completing my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, my foundation is deeply rooted in high-performance computing, AI-driven software architecture, and autonomous systems."

The Track Record

"Before pivoting to the cultural economy, I spent my time in the trenches of deep tech. I am the architect behind AirLance (an autonomous UAV medical delivery ecosystem) and ZeroBite (a food waste logistics platform). From being a finalist at the Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2024) to developing advanced SaaS platforms like StenoAI, I understand what it takes to take a complex idea from a blank blueprint to a fully deployed reality."

The Art Startup Vision

"Now, under the umbrella of Gen Solution, we are turning our focus to one of India’s most historically rich yet digitally fragmented sectors: the Art Market. We are not just building another website; we are building a high-speed e-commerce engine utilizing the MERN stack and Flutter. Our goal is to democratize art—bridging the gap between passionate local creators, art students, and the booming Indian consumer base. We are building the infrastructure that makes art accessible, from minor clay crafts to premium diamond pieces."

Why Join My Team?

"I am not looking for traditional academic scorecards or textbook answers. I am looking for raw talent, relentless drive, and the ability to execute under pressure. We operate in rapid-deployment sprints. We move fast, we analyze deep data, and we build products that scale. If you have the hustle to map out markets, the technical chops to build seamless UI/UX, or the strategic mind to hack growth, and if you are ready to be part of a team that works as hard as it dreams—you belong here. Let’s build the future of Indian art commerce together."
